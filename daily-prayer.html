Daily Prayer

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 25, 2026

Khoulach ("we", "us", or "our") built the Daily Prayer app as a free, ad-supported application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our app. By using Daily Prayer, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information Collection

Daily Prayer is designed with your privacy in mind. The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. You use the app completely anonymously.

The limited data we handle includes:

  • Your device's approximate location (used solely for prayer time calculations)
  • App preferences and usage data stored locally on your device
  • Anonymous advertising identifiers used by Google AdMob to serve ads

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on external servers.

2. Location Data

Daily Prayer requests access to your device's location in order to calculate accurate prayer times based on your geographic position. This location data is:

  • Used only on your device for prayer time calculations
  • Never transmitted to our servers or any third party
  • Never stored beyond the current session unless you choose to save a default location

You may deny location permission at any time through your device's settings. If location access is denied, you can manually set your city or coordinates within the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Daily Prayer displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support the free availability of the app. Google AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device advertising identifiers
  • General device information (device model, operating system version)
  • IP address (used by Google to estimate general location for ad targeting)

This data collection is governed by Google's own privacy policy. For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob & Ads Data Collection .

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads on Android devices).

4. Data Storage

All app data, including your preferences, saved prayer records, and settings, is stored locally on your device using SQLite. This means:

  • Your data remains on your device at all times
  • No personal data is sent to or stored on external servers
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data

The app integrates Supabase as a backend service for potential future features. As of the current version, no user data is transmitted to Supabase or any remote database.

5. Push Notifications

Daily Prayer may send you push notifications to remind you of upcoming prayer times. These notifications are:

  • Scheduled and triggered locally on your device
  • Not used for advertising or marketing purposes
  • Fully controllable through your device's notification settings

You can enable or disable notifications at any time from within the app or through your device's system settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Daily Prayer is suitable for users of all ages. The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since no personal data or account creation is required, the app can be safely used by children.

Advertisements served through Google AdMob comply with applicable regulations regarding ads shown to children. If you believe that a child has somehow provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable laws. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
