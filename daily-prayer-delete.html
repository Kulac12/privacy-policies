Data Deletion Request

How Your Data is Stored

Daily Prayer stores all data locally on your device. No personal data is sent to or stored on any external server. This means you have full control over your data at all times.

How to Delete Your Data

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it by following these simple steps:

Option 1: Uninstall the App
Uninstalling Daily Prayer from your device will permanently delete all locally stored data, including prayer records, dhikr counts, Quran reading progress, garden state, and all settings.
Option 2: Clear App Data
Go to your device Settings → Apps → Daily Prayer → Storage → Clear Data. This will reset the app to its initial state and delete all stored data.

What Data is Deleted

  • Prayer tracking records
  • Dhikr (zikir) counter history
  • Quran reading and memorization progress
  • Garden state, Nur points, and achievements
  • Streak history
  • App preferences and settings
  • All locally cached data

Third-Party Data

Google AdMob may collect anonymized advertising identifiers. To reset or opt out of personalized advertising, go to your device Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID or Opt Out.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.
